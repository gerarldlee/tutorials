core use cases:
======================
know your location in the maps or starting point

ask where your destination is

display available parking spaces in that area
	display the reservation prices

select parking destination in maps
	highlights the available area borders in the picture
	displays the route from starting point to this area

display parking destination area in maps
	along with info about how much to pay to reserve
	how many hours
	reservation rate per hour / min, an area can be reservable or not depends on the owner
	booking rate per hour / min
	reserve or cancel
	book or end
	who is the owner
	display photos
	has stars + comments / feedback

on-street parking and car parks are not reservable (car parks can also be b2b clients )

booking a parking space
	has a different rate than reserving it.
	booking starts with the moment you arrive, ends with the moment you leave, or per hour


a building / parking building can be mapped in 3D


other use cases:
======================
users can view their bookings / billings

as a parking space owner, you can rent your space
